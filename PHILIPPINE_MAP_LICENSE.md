# Philippine geographic boundary artwork — attribution

The 18-region SVG paths in `src/philippineRegionPaths.js` are derived from the TopoJSON file `latest/topojson/country/lowres/country.topo.0.001.json` in [faeldon/philippines-json-maps](https://github.com/faeldon/philippines-json-maps), reused from the Philippine Economic Intelligence Dashboard. Polygon paths have been simplified and projected to the portfolio card's SVG coordinate space. The outlines are geographic illustrations, not official boundary determinations.
